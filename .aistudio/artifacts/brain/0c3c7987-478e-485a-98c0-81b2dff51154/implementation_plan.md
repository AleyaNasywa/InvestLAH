## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: A 9-module interactive financial literacy playbook for Malaysian university students featuring an inflation time machine, Tinder-style risk profiler, cashflow & PTPTN converter, 3-step goal planner, investment race simulator, asset classifier, 3D flip dictionary, Syariah screener & dividend detox calculator, and a 10-question Final Boss Quiz.
- **Target Audience / Persona**: Gen-Z Malaysian students learning personal finance and investing from scratch through punchy, zero-jargon Manglish (`pokai`, `healing`, `terciduk`, `stonks`, `noob`).
- **Key Value**: Replaces sleepy pastel gradients with a crisp, wide-awake, gender-neutral Neo-Brutalist UI that keeps students energized while mastering real-world financial skills.

## 2. User Experience & Visual Design

  - *Aesthetic Direction*: Sharp, industrial, high-energy street-smart aesthetic with zero dreamy mesh gradients, zero pink/purple pastel washes, and zero dark mode.
  - *Color Palette & Mood*:
    - **60% Dominant Neutral Canvas**: Crisp Cool Ash Gray (`#F3F4F6`) global background with stark Solid White (`#FFFFFF`) primary cards.
    - **30% Structural Surfaces & Borders**: Aggressive `3px` solid black borders (`#000000`), harsh offset drop shadows (`6px 6px 0px #000000`), and clean slate dividers.
    - **10% High-Contrast Accents (Gender-Neutral & Raw)**:
      - Electric Matcha Green (`#10D989`)
      - Cyber Coral / Bold Red (`#FF4766`)

      - Bright Warning Yellow (`#FDE047`)
      - Stark Orange (`#FB923C`)

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: High-Contrast Solid Surfaces Over Soft Pastels**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Replace all gradient washes (`from-[#ECFDF5]`, pink/lavender cards, cream backdrops) with `#F3F4F6` (Cool Ash Gray) canvas, `#FFFFFF` cards, and bold solid accent blocks (`#10D989`, `#3B82F6`, `#FDE047`, `#FF4766`, `#FB923C`).
  - *Why*: Eliminates visual fatigue and the overly soft/girly feel while strengthening readability and Neo-Brutalist punch.
- **Decision 2: Confetti Reserved for Tab 9 Final Boss Graduation**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Tabs 1–8 use snappy physical button presses, emoji pops, Web Audio cheers, and CSS shakes (`@keyframes shake`), while the full-screen confetti cannon is reserved for completing the Tab 9 Final Boss Quiz.
  - *Why*: Makes graduating from the Final Boss Quiz feel like a true milestone reward.

### Interactive Component & State Mapping
- **Module Badges (8 Core Badges → Tab 9 Gate)**: Visiting/completing activities across Tabs 1–8 awards up to 8 module badges (`Boba Survivor`, `Risk Checked`, `Budget Boss`, `Goal Getter`, `Time Traveler`, `Asset Master`, `Lingo Master`, `Halal Hunter`). Tab 9 checks this 8-badge array (or the instant `devUnlocked` toggle) to unlock the 10-question Boss Quiz.
- **Tab 9 Review & Share Flows**:
  - Clicking **Review Salah Kat Mana 🔍** toggles an expandable breakdown list of all 10 questions showing the user's selected answer, the correct answer, and the Manglish street-smart explanation.
  - Clicking **Share Result 📲** copies a formatted score summary to the clipboard and displays an inline confirmation toast (`Result dah copy! Paste kat WhatsApp/IG Story kau 🔥`).
